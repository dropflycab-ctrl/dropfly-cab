## 🧳 Passenger & Luggage Capacity
Shared Cab: 3 passengers, each 1 trolley bag (15 kg) + 1 backpack (5 kg) | Sedan: 4 passengers, 2 trolley bags + 2 backpacks | SUV: 5 passengers, 3 trolley bags + 2 backpacks.


## 💳 Payment Information

Customers pay the trip fare directly to the driver.

Available payment methods:
- Cash
- UPI
